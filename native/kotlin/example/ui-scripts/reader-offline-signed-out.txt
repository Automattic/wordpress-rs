// Offline reading, as the reader who isn't signed in: save two posts, download their text and images, and read
// one from the device. Start from a fresh install, so that neither post is saved yet
// (`adb shell pm clear rs.wordpress.example`).
//
// Run it with scripts/example-app-adb-script.py, which lists the steps.

start
wait 2
tap Reader (signed out)
wait 5
tap Freshly Pressed
waitfor #reader_post 40
tapnow 0:~Actions for
wait 1
shot 01-post-menu
tapnow Save
wait 2
tapnow 1:~Actions for
wait 1
tapnow Save
wait 2
back
tap Offline reading
wait 3
shot 02-offline-before-download
tapnow #offline_download_text
wait 6
shot 03-offline-after-text
tapnow #offline_download_images
wait 5
waitgone #reader_busy 120
wait 1
shot 04-offline-after-images
tap 0:#offline_post
wait 5
shot 05-offline-copy
down 3
shot 06-offline-copy-scrolled
