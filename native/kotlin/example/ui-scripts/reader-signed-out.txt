// The Reader's screens, as the reader who isn't signed in. Nothing here changes an account: the tag it follows
// stays on the device, and it unfollows it again.
//
// Run it with scripts/example-app-adb-script.py, which lists the steps.

start
wait 2
tap Reader (signed out)
wait 6
shot 01-home
// A tag, followed on this device only, and unfollowed again.
tap Follow tag art-design
wait 3
shot 02-tag-followed
tap Unfollow tag art-design
wait 2
// Discover can open with a card of sites that fills the screen, so the first post is scrolled to.
tap Discover
waitfor #reader_stream_status 40
wait 4
shot 03-discover
tap 0:#reader_post
wait 6
shot 04-post
back
back
tap Freshly Pressed
waitfor #reader_post 40
down 6
shot 05-freshly-pressed-scrolled
back
tap First Posts
wait 8
shot 06-first-posts
back
tap Saved posts
wait 3
shot 07-saved
back
tap Offline reading
wait 3
shot 08-offline
back
tap Subscriptions
wait 4
shot 09-subscriptions
back
tap Blocked sites
wait 4
shot 10-blocked-sites
back
tap Find sites
wait 3
shot 11-find-sites
